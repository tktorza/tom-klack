How I work

Same method, every time

01

Listen to everyone

Founders, staff, engineers, customers, ex-partners. Each one holds a piece of the answer.

02

Find the one real cause

Not the loudest theory. The thing that, once fixed, unlocks the rest.

03

Cut scope, ship a sprint

Freeze what doesn't matter, deliver what does, fast. Results in days or weeks, not quarters.

04

Make it run without me

The right people, tools and habits in place, so it keeps moving after I leave.
